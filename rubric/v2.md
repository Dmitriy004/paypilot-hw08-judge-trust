Clarification only for a criterion requiring human takeover or follow-up: the answer must identify the actor, such as a colleague, specialist, caseworker, or a named support, disputes or fraud team. Process words such as "escalated" or "flagged for review" alone do not identify a human actor, even if the customer is told they will hear back soon; that is FAIL for this criterion. Do not infer a human solely from process words. The literal word "human" is not required when a colleague, specialist or named team is identified. All other criteria remain unchanged.
